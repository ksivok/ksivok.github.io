Chicken Yeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes how Ksivok Games handles information in the Android / Google Play version of Chicken Yeet (package com.ap.ksivok.games.chickenyeet). Other games and web-portal versions may use different platform services and are not covered by this policy.

1. Game progress and Google Play Games

Chicken Yeet stores progress and preferences on your device, including coins, upgrades, records, tutorial progress, language, and sound settings. Local play and progress remain available without Google Play Games sign-in or a network connection. The game does not use a Ksivok Games server to synchronize progress and does not integrate Firebase Analytics.

The Android game uses Google Play Games Services. It attempts automatic sign-in with your Play Games profile and offers a manual sign-in option when needed. When you are signed in and the service is available, the game can save and restore a snapshot of your progress and preferences through Google's Saved Games service in the game's private application data area in your Google Drive account.

The game uses your Play Games player ID and display name to identify the signed-in profile and associate progress with that profile. It submits your highest flight distance, as an integer number of metres, to the Highest Flight leaderboard. Your player profile and score may be visible to other players according to your Play Games profile privacy settings.

Google Play Games Services also processes gamer identity, analytics, and diagnostic information for its service and SDK, as described in Google's Play Games Services data disclosures. See also Google's Saved Games documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Device or operating-system backup services may separately back up app data according to your device and account settings.

2. Advertising services

The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for interstitial and optional rewarded video ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices.

The ads SDK may process an IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), advertising ID and other device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. These data support advertising, measurement/analytics, and fraud prevention. This is third-party SDK processing even though the game does not have its own account system or analytics backend.

For Google's practices, see the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

3. Purposes and recipients

Game progress is used to operate the game, restore progress on your device, and synchronize it through Google Saved Games when signed in. Play Games profile information identifies the account associated with that progress, and flight records are used for leaderboard rankings. Google processes the cloud saves, Play Games profile information, and leaderboard records through its services. Advertising information is processed by Google and the advertising participants described in Google's notices and any applicable consent form. We do not operate a service that sells your save file or uploads it to our own server.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

4. Your choices

You can play locally without signing in to Google Play Games; cloud synchronization and online leaderboard features require sign-in and service availability. You can manage the account used for supported games, automatic sign-in, and who can view your Play Games profile in Google's Play Games profile settings.

Where required, the game presents Google's advertising privacy message. Where UMP requires an in-game privacy-options entry, it is available from the game menu so you can revisit eligible advertising choices. Your choices may affect ad availability. Rewarded videos are optional; you can decline an offer and continue playing without its reward.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and manage advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Availability and names of these controls depend on your Android version. Changing advertising preferences does not necessarily stop all SDK processing or all ads.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete local progress by clearing Chicken Yeet's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This does not itself delete cloud saves, your Play Games profile, or leaderboard records held by Google. Signing in again may restore cloud progress. Separately manage any device backups if you do not want them to restore app data.

To manage or delete Google Play Games data for Chicken Yeet or your Play Games profile, use Google's account controls and instructions for deleting Play Games data. Google determines retention for information it processes under its own policies; deleting Play Games data does not necessarily delete information held in other Google services, such as Play History or advertising records. Use Google's privacy controls and contact channels for those requests.

For support correspondence or questions about access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights, contact us below. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

6. Security and international processing

According to Google's SDK disclosures, Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and Play Games Services data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Local progress relies on Android's app storage protections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google may process information in countries other than your own, subject to its privacy policy and applicable safeguards. Where consent is required for advertising processing, Google's privacy message offers the applicable choices.

7. Intended audience

Chicken Yeet is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. A store content rating is separate from this intended audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess and address the request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current policy will be available on this page with its updated date.
