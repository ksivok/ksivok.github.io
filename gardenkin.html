A cozy garden restoration game

Gardenkin

Grow a garden. Bring people together.

Clear the overgrown paths of a forgotten community garden and make room for something new. Plant your first crops, repair its quiet corners by hand, and get to know the neighbours who help bring it back to life.

More than a harvest

Make this place feel alive again

01

Restore it with your own hands

Clear paths, repair fences, raise an arch, and build welcoming corners. Watch each small piece of work leave a visible mark on the garden.

02

Grow, gather, and make

Care for vegetables, flowers, and fruit trees. Store your harvest, sell what you can spare, and turn ingredients into preserves for your neighbours.

03

Get to know the neighbours

Meet Mira, Theo, June, Rosa, Eli, Basil, and Nora. Their requests and stories give the garden a purpose beyond the next purchase.

04

Find a little helping paw

Rabbit helpers carry ripe produce to storage, while a hedgehog helps with surplus. They handle the routine; the important story moments are yours.

05

A place to keep returning to

Bring everyone together to celebrate the garden's reopening, then keep growing, improving the garden, and taking on new orders.

06

A gentle kind of progress

No combat and no energy meter. Spend time tending plants, helping someone out, or simply enjoying the place you are making together.

From an independent developer

Still growing

Gardenkin is in active development, with Windows as the first planned Steam release. The game supports English and Russian. A release date, price, and store link have not been announced here yet.
